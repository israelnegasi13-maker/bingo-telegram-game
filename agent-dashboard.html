📋 How to Add Referrals Manually

Step 1: Find User Information

  • Ask the user for their Telegram username (e.g., @username)
  • Or ask them to share their User ID from the game bot
  • User must have played at least once in Bingo Elite or Keno Ultra

Step 2: Add User as Referral

  • Type the username/ID in the search box above
  • Click "Add as My Referral" to assign them to you
  • You'll earn commissions from their future wins instantly

Commission Structure:

  • Bingo Wins: 40% commission
  • Keno Wins: 10% commission
  • Minimum Commission: 0.01 ETB per win
  • Real-time: Commissions credited immediately
💡 Pro Tip: Use the "User Suggestions" section to find active players without agents.
